## Why New Zealand businesses look for integration help

New Zealand has one of the most connected small business software ecosystems in the world, much of it built locally. Xero, Cin7 and Unleashed all started here. Most businesses run several of these tools side by side. The difficulty is rarely the individual app. It is keeping orders, stock, invoices and payments consistent between them as the business grows, adds channels or starts exporting.

## How I work with New Zealand businesses

I work remotely with owners and operations and finance managers across New Zealand. We start by mapping how information moves between your systems today and where staff fill the gaps by hand. Then we agree which improvements are worth making first. Delivery runs in small stages, each tested with realistic data and reviewed with you.

Where an existing Xero app or connector does the job, I will say so. Custom integration and software are for the cases where they clearly pay for themselves.
