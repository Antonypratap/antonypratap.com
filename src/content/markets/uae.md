## Why UAE businesses outgrow their systems

UAE businesses tend to grow quickly and across channels: a showroom, a website, marketplaces, delivery apps and a sales team on WhatsApp, often across more than one entity. Each channel usually arrives with its own software. The result is familiar: orders re-typed into the accounting system, stock that does not match between the website and the warehouse, and finance teams reconciling exports at month end.

Regulation adds to the pressure. VAT, Corporate Tax and the move to e-invoicing all depend on records that are complete and consistent across systems. That is difficult when the systems do not talk to each other.
