## Why work with someone based in Bangalore

Some of the most valuable work in a systems project happens in the room: watching how orders are really processed, where the spreadsheets live and which steps people work around. Being based in Bangalore makes it straightforward to spend that time on site when a project needs it, while the build itself runs remotely and efficiently.

## How I work with businesses in India

A project starts with a conversation, then a short discovery exercise that maps how information moves between your systems today and where it breaks. Delivery runs in stages, each one tested with realistic data and reviewed with you before it touches live operations. You have one accountable point of contact who understands both the business requirement and the technical work.
