## The client and the problem

OpsFirst was built for iDine, to serve iDine's own clients. A service provider working for many client organisations needs to manage each client's requests in an organised way: who the request is for, which part of the client's organisation it concerns, which team should handle it and how it progresses.

## What the platform covers

OpsFirst is a provider-side platform: it is used by the service provider to deliver and manage services for its clients. Its capabilities include:

- **Customer accounts** for each client organisation served.
- **Departments and teams** to reflect how the client and the provider are organised.
- **Categories** to classify the work.
- **Issue routing** so each request reaches the team responsible for it.
- **Operational workflows** to take requests from logging to resolution.
