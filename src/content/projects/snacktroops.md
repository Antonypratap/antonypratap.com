## The concept

SnackTroops is a school-based initiative in the United Arab Emirates, built around hampers. Children take part in hamper-related activities and their management, and in doing so learn practical leadership and sales skills.

## Why it is a distinctive project

The concept combines schools, children, a physical product and an educational purpose. A digital platform for it has to fit how schools and families actually work, and how the hamper activities are organised and managed day to day, rather than following a standard software pattern.

## How the work came about

The project came to me through a referral, a reminder that the best work often comes from trust built in earlier relationships.
