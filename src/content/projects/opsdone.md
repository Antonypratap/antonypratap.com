## The business problem

In businesses that operate across several locations, operational problems are often reported by phone, messaging apps and email. Issues are forgotten, nobody is clearly responsible, and managers cannot see what is open, what is late or where the same problem keeps recurring.

## What OpsDone does

OpsDone gives operational work a single place to be recorded, owned and followed through, across locations.

- **Issue management.** Operational issues are logged with an issue type and a priority.
- **Ownership.** Every issue has a responsible owner.
- **Response-time targets.** Issues carry targets for response, so overdue work is visible.
- **Escalation.** Work that misses its target is escalated.
- **Activity tracking.** Each issue keeps a history of what happened and who did it.
- **Attachments and work logs.** Photos, documents and notes of the work done are kept with the issue.
