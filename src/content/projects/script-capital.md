## The decision

Script Capital needed a website, and with it a decision that shapes cost, speed and maintenance for years: which platform to build it on.

## The advice

I advised Script Capital on the technology for the website and recommended Framer, a hosted website platform. For a business website that needs to look polished, load quickly and be updated without developers, a hosted platform removes the hosting, security updates and plugin maintenance that a self-managed site brings.

The reasoning behind this kind of recommendation is explained in [Framer or WordPress? How to choose a platform for your business website](/insights/framer-vs-wordpress-business-website/).
