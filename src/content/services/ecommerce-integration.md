## The website is only the front of the operation

An ecommerce website that is not connected to operations creates work. Every order needs to be re-keyed, every stock change updated by hand and every payment matched to an order. As volume grows, the manual work grows with it, and so do the errors customers notice.

## What I look at

- **Products and prices.** Where they are maintained, and how changes reach every channel.
- **Stock.** The system of record, update frequency, reservations and what customers see when stock runs out.
- **Orders.** How they reach fulfilment, how status flows back, and how changes and cancellations are handled.
- **Payments.** Gateways, refunds, reconciliation with orders and invoices, and how failures are handled.
- **Customers.** Accounts, trade terms and data that sales and service teams need.

## How the work is delivered

The integration is designed alongside the website, not after it. Each flow is built and tested separately with realistic data, so the business can switch flows on one at a time.
