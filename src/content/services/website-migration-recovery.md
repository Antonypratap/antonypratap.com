## Why businesses get stuck on expensive platforms

Hosted website and gallery platforms make it easy to start. Over the years the subscription grows, the business uses a fraction of the features, and the content (especially large image libraries) feels too big and too risky to move. The longer it stays, the harder leaving looks.

A website can also disappear altogether: a lapsed payment, a closed account, an expired domain or a supplier who is no longer around. Without a clear picture of who controls what, getting it back can feel impossible.

## How I approach it

The work starts by establishing what the business actually controls and what can be recovered. Only then is a plan made: where the site will live, what it will cost to run, and how the move protects existing URLs and search visibility. The switch happens once the new site is complete and checked, so visitors see a working site throughout.
